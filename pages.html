AICL Website Prototype

All 48 pages of the restructured website, clickable end to end. The design keeps AICL's current look; every page leads to one next step: book a free consultation, chat on WhatsApp, or get a free guide.

Open the homepageTry the Cost Estimator

Things to try

  • Book a consultation and switch between Ikeja, Alapere and online.
  • Run the Eligibility Checker with an HND and Canada.
  • Stay on any content page for 15 seconds, or scroll half-way, to see the free-guide slide-in. It shows once per visit; the purple Free guide tab reopens it.
  • Open a FAQ answer, or switch Blog & News between guides and news.
  • Turn the yellow pipeline notes on or off with the button at the bottom of each page.

Before this goes live

  • Pages are laid out at desktop width (1440px). Phone layouts are the web designer's next step.
  • Text in [square brackets] is a placeholder for AICL to supply: opening hours, event dates, fees, photos, maps and article text.
  • Cost Estimator figures are samples. Real figures need sources and an “as of” date.
  • Forms are not connected yet; every submit opens the thank-you page.
